ALEMONA — Natural Hair & Scalp Care

Luxury Interactive Digital Product Catalog / Brand Showcase

Design and build a premium, high-end interactive digital product catalog for ALEMONA Natural Hair & Scalp Care.

This is NOT an e-commerce website.

The purpose of the website is to function as a sophisticated digital showroom where customers can explore ALEMONA's hair-care products, packaging, ingredients, textures, benefits, and usage information.

There must be NO prices, shopping cart, checkout, payment functionality, Buy Now buttons, product purchasing, or e-commerce elements anywhere in the interface.

The overall experience should feel like entering the digital showroom of a luxury botanical hair-care brand.

---

1. CORE VISUAL DIRECTION

The visual identity must be based heavily on the actual ALEMONA product packaging.

The brand aesthetic is:

Luxury Botanical · Premium · Earthy · Sophisticated · Natural · Clean · Modern · High-End

The packaging uses a sophisticated combination of:

- Deep forest green
- Dark botanical green
- Charcoal / deep gray
- Warm bronze
- Antique gold
- Metallic gold
- Muted terracotta / bronze
- Warm cream
- Natural earthy tones

Do NOT make the website look bright, colorful, playful, overly feminine, clinical, or like a generic organic skincare website.

The design should feel closer to a luxury fragrance, premium cosmetics, or high-end salon brand presentation.

Use generous negative space, elegant typography, subtle metallic accents, refined shadows, sophisticated gradients, and cinematic product presentation.

Avoid excessive decoration.

Luxury should come from spacing, typography, photography, material textures, lighting, and restraint rather than adding unnecessary graphic elements.
